Design sketches, 13 November 2026

Four ways to make the site look like people built it.

All four show the same top half of the home page: headline, numbers, customers, technology selector, a testimonial, the call to action. Only the look changes.

What stays untouchedCopy, headings, their order and levels. Everything that keeps the site ranking in Google and AI search.
What comes from the current siteThe red and black, Inter Tight and DM Sans, the logo, your own team and event photos.
What is goneThe red glow, the node diagram, the counting numbers. Those are the cues that make a page read as AI-made.

A. People first

Light background. Three real photos laid out like prints replace the diagram, one word underlined by hand. Technologies as a list, not cards.

References on Mobbin: Clay, Maze, Dovetail

B. Proof mosaic

A grid right under the headline: customer logos, named quotes, result numbers and photos. Proof before the pitch.

References on Mobbin: Intercom, Jasper, Contractbook

C. Editorial

White, very large type, hairlines, numbered sections. Photos in black and white, colour returns on hover. Technologies open as a list.

References on Mobbin: Harvey, Ploy

D. Dark, warmer

Closest to what is live now. The dark hero stays, a photo wall replaces the diagram, numbers sit in one calm row, quotes sit on photos.

References on Mobbin: Retool, Sana
